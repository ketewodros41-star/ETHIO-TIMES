# ETHIOTIMES Instagram Theme System

This document outlines the visual theme system used for rendering Instagram posts via Playwright in the backend.

## Themes

### verified_brief
- **Label**: Verified Brief
- **Accent**: Green
- **Intent**: Evidenced news brief — confirmed event with primary sources
- **Status**: Production
- **Use cases**: High-confidence news reports that have verified primary sources.
- **Example**: Major policy changes, verified economic announcements.

### breaking
- **Label**: Breaking
- **Accent**: Red
- **Intent**: Breaking news — signal red FlashBar, high urgency
- **Status**: Production
- **Use cases**: Urgent, developing news requiring immediate attention.
- **Example**: Sudden geopolitical events, major accidents.

### politics_sensitive
- **Label**: Politics
- **Accent**: Gold
- **Intent**: Political stories — review-gated, amber accent
- **Status**: Stub
- **Use cases**: Sensitive political coverage requiring careful editorial review.
- **Example**: Elections, diplomatic disputes.

### economy
- **Label**: Economy
- **Accent**: Green
- **Intent**: Economic & financial news
- **Status**: Stub
- **Use cases**: Market updates, corporate news, fiscal policy.
- **Example**: Currency rate updates, new central bank policies.

### data_chart
- **Label**: Data
- **Accent**: Gold
- **Intent**: Data-driven visual — statistics and charts
- **Status**: Stub
- **Use cases**: Data-heavy stories requiring visual infographics.
- **Example**: Census data, demographic shifts, economic indices.

### official_statement
- **Label**: Official
- **Accent**: Gold
- **Intent**: Government or institutional statements
- **Status**: Stub
- **Use cases**: Direct broadcasts of institutional messaging.
- **Example**: Press releases, institutional announcements.

### quote
- **Label**: Quote
- **Accent**: Green
- **Intent**: Pull-quote format for key statements
- **Status**: Stub
- **Use cases**: Highlighting impactful statements from key figures.
- **Example**: Interviews, speeches.

### culture_photo
- **Label**: Culture
- **Accent**: Gold
- **Intent**: Full-bleed cultural and arts coverage
- **Status**: Stub
- **Use cases**: Human interest, arts, culture, lifestyle.
- **Example**: Festivals, artist profiles.
